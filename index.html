Welcome to Evolve

A Studio Built Around Trust

Evolve Dance and Fitness is being created for local families who want quality instruction close to home. Led by President and Lead Instructor Kate Gefael, the program emphasizes youth dance fundamentals, safe movement, age-appropriate programming, parent communication, and a positive studio culture.

Professional Studio Experience

Our launch planning includes a dedicated dance surface, mirror wall, clean check-in flow, and reliable systems designed to support student safety, parent communication, and efficient studio operations.

Purposeful Instruction

Programming is designed to help students build strong fundamentals, confidence, teamwork, and love of movement through classes such as ballet, hip hop, tap, jazz/pom, technique, dance fitness, and line dancing.

Programs Preview

Classes for Kids, Teens, and Adults

From early childhood movement to youth dance foundations and adult fitness options, Evolve is developing a simple, family-friendly class lineup for the Molalla community.

Early Childhood

Tiny Hoppers and Pre-Dance classes for rhythm, coordination, creativity, and joyful movement.

Youth Dance

Ballet, hip hop, jazz/pom, tap, technique, leaps and turns, and related progression-based classes.

Teen & Adult Fitness

Accessible, high-energy dance fitness and line dancing options for teens and adults.
