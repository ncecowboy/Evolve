Our Story

Kate has taught with several other studios in the past and has had a dream to have her own studio where she can provide her special care and compassion for children and dance. As her position was ending at one studio, Kate met up with Josh to discuss her possible opportuntities for opening up a new studio. As long time friends, Kate and Josh rely on each other for advice and reasoning. A few days later, Josh considered Kate's passion and knowledge for Dance and asked her if she wanted to have a real, true partner in the business. Kate almost jumped at the opportunity, and soon after, Evolve Dance and Fitness was born. To her husband's dismay, the pair plan to open the studio in Molalla, Oregon instead of Brightwood, Oregon as he would have liked, but he is very supportive of the efforts to get Evolve up and running.

Premium Facility

Dance safely on our custom-engineered, 1,000+ square-foot professional sprung dance floor. Featuring a specialized puck system and a premium Marley surface, the studio is built to prevent injuries and elevate performance.

Under Kate's direction, our programming blends rigorous technical training with an accessible, empowering atmosphere for the entire community.
