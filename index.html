Welcome to Evolve

Opening in 2026, Evolve Dance and Fitness is on a mission to make dance and fitness accessible to local youth. Led by President and Lead Instructor Kate Gefael, our program is designed to build confidence, strong character, and lifelong wellness in a safe, uplifting, and highly structured environment.

About Us

Kate Gefael

President & Co-Founder

Kate is the co-founder of Evolve Dance & Fitness, a wife, proud mother of four daughters, and a trusted Realtor with Better Homes and Gardens Realty Partners. Having danced since the age of two, dance has been a lifelong passion and an important part of her life for as long as she can remember.

With over eight years of experience coaching, choreographing, and mentoring young dancers, Kate is dedicated to creating a positive environment where children can grow both on and off the dance floor. Throughout her coaching career, she has volunteered her time to choreograph and coach local youth sports and dance programs, helping keep opportunities available for young athletes in the community. Under Kate's choreography and direction, teams have earned state championship titles and numerous competitive achievements. While she is proud of those accomplishments, she believes the greatest success comes from helping children develop confidence, discipline, teamwork, leadership, and lasting friendships.

Kate's teaching philosophy centers around strong fundamentals, proper technique, and age-appropriate training. She believes dancers should build a solid foundation and progress at a steady pace, allowing them to develop skills correctly while maintaining their love for dance.

As co-founder of Evolve Dance & Fitness, Kate helped create a studio focused on accessibility, affordability, and community. Her goal is to provide a welcoming and supportive space where every dancer feels encouraged, empowered, and inspired to become the best version of themselves. Whether she's helping families find their dream home or helping young dancers find confidence in themselves, Kate is passionate about serving her community and making a positive impact in the lives of others. For Kate, dance is about more than trophies and titles, it's about building confidence, creating lifelong memories, and teaching skills that will benefit children far beyond their years in the studio.

Josh Kayser

Vice President & Co-Founder

Josh Kayser is the Vice President and Co-Founder of Evolve Dance & Fitness, bringing a wealth of operational and technical expertise to the studio. Drawing on his extensive career as the Operations Manager for the Supply Solutions Group at North Coast Electric, Josh ensures the day-to-day business runs seamlessly. His hands-on leadership extends to every corner of Evolve—from establishing the studio’s digital infrastructure and secure registration systems to personally designing and engineering the custom 1,000+ square-foot professional sprung dance floor to guarantee a safe, premium surface for all students.

A dedicated fixture in Molalla for nearly 30 years through his volunteer work with the Molalla Buckeroo, Josh has deep roots in the community. While he calls Oregon City home—where he actively serves as Chair of the Citizen Involvement Committee and the Hillendale Neighborhood Association representative—his commitment to supporting local youth and families spans the entire region.

As a proud father of a 21-year-old daughter, Josh is a passionate advocate for youth. He understands firsthand the importance of creating safe, structured, and empowering environments where kids can thrive. Recognizing Kate’s undeniable passion and instructional talent, Josh proposed a true partnership to bring her vision to life. He consistently shows up to empower children, ensuring they have the support, resources, and top-tier facilities they need to succeed in whatever they do.

Our Story

Evolve Dance and Fitness was born from a shared vision between long-time friends Kate and Josh. After teaching at several other studios, Kate dreamed of opening a space where she could fully realize her compassionate approach to dance instruction. Recognizing her undeniable passion and industry knowledge, Josh proposed a true partnership to bring her vision to life. By combining Kate's instructional talent with Josh's operational background, Evolve was established to serve the Molalla community with excellence.

Our Offerings

We are building a comprehensive schedule to serve the entire community. Here is a look at what we are bringing to the studio:

Early Childhood

Designed for ages 3-5, our Tiny Hoppers and Pre-Dance classes focus on rhythm, coordination, and the joy of movement, providing a perfect foundation for our youngest dancers.

Core Youth Dance

A structured progression for ages 6 to teens. Offerings will include Ballet, Hip Hop, Jazz/Pom, Tap, and specialized Leaps & Turns / Technique classes to build strong, capable dancers.

Adult & Teen Fitness

Keep moving with our high-energy Dance Fitness and popular Line Dancing classes. These formats are designed to be accessible, fun, and a great workout for ages 14 and up.

Flexible Options

Not sure where to start? We plan to offer Trial Classes so youth dancers can find the perfect fit before committing. For our adult fitness programs, convenient Drop-In options will be available.
