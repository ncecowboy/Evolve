Welcome to Evolve

Opening in 2026, Evolve Dance and Fitness is dedicated to providing high-quality instruction in a supportive, high-energy environment. Led by President and Lead Instructor Kate Gefael, our classes are designed to build technique, confidence, and a lifelong love of movement.

Our Team

Kate Gefael – President. A long-time dance mom and passionate instructor, Kate brings her special care, compassion, and extensive experience to every class to ensure every student thrives.

Josh Kayser – Vice President. Bringing a wealth of operational expertise and a deep commitment to the community, Josh ensures the day-to-day business runs seamlessly. While he calls Oregon City home—where he serves as Chair of the Citizen Involvement Committee—Josh has been a dedicated fixture in Molalla for nearly 30 years through his volunteer work with the Molalla Buckeroo. Drawing on his career as an Operations Manager, he is a passionate advocate for youth, consistently showing up to empower kids and provide the resources they need to succeed.

Our Story

Evolve Dance and Fitness was born from a shared vision between long-time friends Kate and Josh. After teaching at several other studios, Kate dreamed of opening a space where she could fully realize her compassionate approach to dance instruction. Recognizing her undeniable passion and industry knowledge, Josh proposed a true partnership to bring her vision to life. By combining Kate's instructional talent with Josh's operational background, Evolve was established to serve the Molalla community with excellence.

Premium Facility

Dance safely on our custom-engineered, 1,000+ square-foot professional sprung dance floor. Featuring a specialized pool-noodle puck system and a premium Marley surface, the studio is built to prevent injuries and elevate performance.

Expert Instruction

Under Kate's direction, our programming blends rigorous technical training with an accessible, empowering atmosphere for the entire community.
