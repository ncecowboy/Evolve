Registration steps

How to Register

1. Create or Access Your Account

Open the official registration link and create or access your family account in Jackrabbit.

2. Complete Required Information

Provide student, family, emergency, and account details requested during registration before choosing classes.

3. Select Classes

Review available classes and choose the best age-appropriate option for your student.

4. Submit Enrollment

Complete the registration process so Evolve can confirm enrollment details and class placement.
