Pajama Dance Party

Parents Night Out • Ages 6-12 • Friday, October 9, 2026

One-Time Special Event

Dance, Play, and Glow

Wear your favorite pajamas and join Evolve Dance and Fitness for a fun Parents Night Out event in Molalla.

This is a recreational drop-off event with no choreographed routine or formal dance instruction. Children will enjoy free dance, freeze dance, a Twister tournament, Figaro's pizza and juice, Dance Four Corners, balloon games, a glow dance party, dance-offs, and limbo.

$35 for the first child, $30 for each additional sibling registered in the same family. Space is limited to 25 children with a waitlist available.
