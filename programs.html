Programs by category

Class Options

Early Childhood Dance

Age-appropriate classes introduce rhythm, movement, coordination, listening skills, and classroom confidence for young dancers.

  • My First Dance and Play, Ages 1-3
  • Tumble Tots, Ages 3-5
  • Mini Movers, Ages 3-5
  • Twirl and Tap, Ages 3-5

Youth Dance Foundations

Foundation classes help students develop technique, musicality, balance, strength, and positive studio habits.

  • Acro Foundations
  • Lyrical Foundations
  • Hip Hop Foundations
  • Tap Foundations
  • Jazz Pom Foundations
  • Contemporary Foundations

Age 9+ Dance Classes

Older students build performance readiness, expression, conditioning, flexibility, and style-specific skills.

  • Lyrical
  • Hip Hop
  • Technique and Conditioning
  • Jazz Pom
  • Contemporary
  • Leaps and Turns

Ballet, Fitness, and Private Lessons

Students and families can choose ballet, line dance fitness, and private lesson availability for solos, duos, and trios.

  • Petite Ballet, Ages 6-8
  • Ballet, Ages 9-12
  • Ballet, Ages 13+
  • Line Dance Fitness, Ages 12+
  • Private lessons available on Fridays
