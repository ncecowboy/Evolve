Programs

Dance and fitness classes designed to help students grow with confidence, structure, age-appropriate instruction, and a love of movement.

Planned Offerings

Evolve is building a balanced schedule for early childhood students, youth dancers, teens, and adults. Final classes will be confirmed as registration and enrollment timing are finalized.

Early Childhood

Designed for young dancers getting started with rhythm, coordination, classroom confidence, and the joy of movement.

  • Tiny Hoppers
  • Pre-Dance
  • Movement foundations

Core Youth Dance

Progression-based classes focused on technique, safety, confidence, teamwork, and strong dance fundamentals.

  • Ballet
  • Hip Hop
  • Jazz / Pom
  • Tap
  • Leaps & Turns / Technique
  • Contemporary / Lyrical options as scheduling allows

Teen & Adult Fitness

Accessible movement options for teens and adults who want a fun, welcoming way to stay active.

  • Dance Fitness
  • Line Dancing
  • Adult dance options as demand develops

Flexible Options

Additional offerings will be added in phases to support student placement, summer programming, and individual goals.

  • Trial classes when available
  • Private / solo lessons as offered
  • Seasonal camps
  • Future events and showcases
Registration note: Class placement and final schedules will depend on enrollment demand, age/level fit, instructor availability, and facility timing.
