Welcome to Evolve

Opening in 2026, Evolve Dance and Fitness is dedicated to providing high-quality instruction in a supportive, high-energy environment. Led by CEO and Lead Instructor Kate Gefeal, our classes are designed to build technique, confidence, and a lifelong love of movement.

Premium Facility

Dance safely on our custom-engineered, 1,200-square-foot professional sprung dance floor. Featuring a specialized puck system and a premium Marley surface, the studio is built to prevent injuries and elevate performance.

Expert Instruction

Under Kate's direction, our programming blends rigorous technical training with an accessible, empowering atmosphere for the entire community.
