Tuition details

How Tuition Works

Registration Fee

The annual registration fee is $30 per student and includes an Evolve Dance T-Shirt.

Monthly Tuition

My First Dance and Play is $50/month. Most recurring weekly classes are $75/month. Fitness classes are $20/class.

4-Class Minimum Billing

Tuition is charged based on a month with at least 4 classes. If there are 5 classes in a given month, there is no additional charge for that fifth class.

Planned Closures

Any planned studio closure will result in a pro-rated tuition amount for that month, so families are only charged for the classes that actually occur.

Multi-Class Discounts

Families enrolling a student in more than one eligible class may receive discounts automatically through the registration system.

Family Discounts

Multi-student discounts are available for families with more than one enrolled student and are automatically applied when eligible.

Tuition FAQ

Common Tuition Questions

Are discounts available?

Yes. Multi-student and/or multi-class discounts are available and automatically applied when eligible.

Is the registration fee monthly?

No. The registration fee is listed separately from monthly tuition and includes an Evolve Dance T-Shirt.

How does tuition work with a 5th class in a month?

Tuition is based on a month with at least four classes. If a month includes five classes, there is no additional charge for that fifth class.

What if the studio closes for a planned holiday or break?

Any planned closure results in a pro-rated tuition amount for that month, so the charge reflects the classes actually held.

Where do I register?

Families can register online through the official Jackrabbit registration link.
