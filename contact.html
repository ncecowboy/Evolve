About Evolve

Locally owned, values-forward, and built to serve Molalla-area families through dance, fitness, confidence, and community.

Our story

Why Evolve

Evolve Dance and Fitness was born from a shared vision between long-time friends Kate and Josh. After teaching at several other studios, Kate dreamed of opening a space where she could fully realize her compassionate approach to dance instruction. Recognizing her passion and industry knowledge, Josh proposed a true partnership to bring her vision to life. By combining Kate's instructional talent with Josh's operational background, Evolve was established to serve the Molalla community with excellence.

Kate Gefael

President & Co-Founder

Kate is the co-founder of Evolve Dance & Fitness, a wife, proud mother of four daughters, and a trusted Realtor with Better Homes and Gardens Realty Partners. Having danced since the age of two, dance has been a lifelong passion and an important part of her life for as long as she can remember.

With over eight years of experience coaching, choreographing, and mentoring young dancers, Kate is dedicated to creating a positive environment where children can grow both on and off the dance floor. Her teaching philosophy centers around strong fundamentals, proper technique, age-appropriate training, confidence, discipline, teamwork, leadership, and lasting friendships.

As co-founder of Evolve Dance & Fitness, Kate helped create a studio focused on accessibility, affordability, and community. Her goal is to provide a welcoming and supportive space where every dancer feels encouraged, empowered, and inspired to become the best version of themselves.

Josh Kayser

Vice President & Co-Founder

Josh Kayser is the Vice President and Co-Founder of Evolve Dance & Fitness, bringing decades of operational, logistical, and technical expertise to the studio. At Evolve, Josh supports day-to-day business operations, digital infrastructure, secure registration systems, facility setup, vendor coordination, and safe, reliable studio operations.

A dedicated fixture in Molalla for nearly 30 years, Josh has deep roots in the community through extensive volunteer work with the Molalla Buckeroo. He also actively serves in Oregon City through local civic and neighborhood involvement.

As a proud father and passionate advocate for youth, Josh understands the importance of creating safe, structured, empowering environments where kids can thrive. His role is to help ensure Evolve has the support, resources, and systems needed to serve families well.

Mission

What Guides Us

To make dance and fitness accessible to local youth by building trust with families through a safe, uplifting environment, thoughtful communication, and age-appropriate programming—fostering confidence, strong character, and lifelong wellness.
