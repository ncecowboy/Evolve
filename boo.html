Two Easy Ways

How to Redeem Your Free Class

Option 1: Reserve Online

Scan the QR code on your card, or use the button below, to open our registration portal and submit a Trial Session for the class you'd like to try.

No payment is required. We'll confirm your spot.

Start a Trial Session

Option 2: Bring Your Card

Register and enroll in advance for the Evolve class you'd like to try, then bring your Boo card with you and hand it to the instructor at check-in.

Dance Fitness classes don't require advance enrollment. Just bring your card.

Not sure which class? Check the schedule or contact us and we'll help.
