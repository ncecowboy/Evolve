Our story

Why We Started Evolve

Evolve Dance and Fitness was born from a shared vision between long-time friends Kate and Josh. After teaching at several other studios, Kate dreamed of opening a space where she could fully realize her compassionate approach to dance instruction. Recognizing her passion and industry knowledge, Josh proposed a true partnership to bring her vision to life. By combining Kate's instructional talent with Josh's operational background, Evolve was established to serve the Molalla community with excellence.

Our goal is to support families in Molalla, Woodburn, Oregon City, Canby, Mulino, Beavercreek, Estacada, Silverton, and surrounding communities with programs that are organized, approachable, and family-centered.

Commitment to Families

We aim to provide thoughtful communication, consistent expectations, and a positive parent experience from registration through the season.

Age-Appropriate Programming

Class content, music, costuming, choreography, and presentations are intended to support a parent-trust standard.

Safety Standards

Studio procedures are built around safe movement, supervision, emergency information, incident reporting, and respectful conduct.

Community Focus

Evolve is built to serve local families and contribute to a positive youth enrichment environment in the Molalla area.

Meet the owners

Leadership

Kate Gefael

President and Co-Founder

Kate is the co-founder of Evolve Dance and Fitness, a wife, proud mother of four daughters, and a trusted Realtor with Better Homes and Gardens Realty Partners. Having danced since the age of two, dance has been a lifelong passion and an important part of her life for as long as she can remember.

With over eight years of experience coaching, choreographing, and mentoring young dancers, Kate is dedicated to creating a positive environment where children can grow both on and off the dance floor. Throughout her coaching career, she has volunteered her time to choreograph and coach local youth sports and dance programs, helping keep opportunities available for young athletes in the community. Under Kate's choreography and direction, teams have earned state championship titles and numerous competitive achievements. While she is proud of those accomplishments, she believes the greatest success comes from helping children develop confidence, discipline, teamwork, leadership, and lasting friendships.

Kate's teaching philosophy centers around strong fundamentals, proper technique, and age-appropriate training. She believes dancers should build a solid foundation and progress at a steady pace, allowing them to develop skills correctly while maintaining their love for dance.

As co-founder of Evolve Dance and Fitness, Kate helped create a studio focused on accessibility, affordability, and community. Her goal is to provide a welcoming and supportive space where every dancer feels encouraged, empowered, and inspired to become the best version of themselves. Whether she's helping families find their dream home or helping young dancers find confidence in themselves, Kate is passionate about serving her community and making a positive impact in the lives of others. For Kate, dance is about more than trophies and titles; it's about building confidence, creating lifelong memories, and teaching skills that will benefit children far beyond their years in the studio.

Josh Kayser

Vice President and Co-Founder

Josh Kayser is the Vice President and Co-Founder of Evolve Dance and Fitness, bringing decades of operational, logistical, and technical expertise to the studio. He oversees business operations, technology infrastructure, registration systems, vendor coordination, strategic planning, and the systems that help create a safe and reliable experience for students and families.

A dedicated fixture in the Molalla community for nearly 30 years, Josh has contributed extensively through his volunteer work with the Molalla Buckeroo since 1997. His involvement has included ticket office management, volunteer coordination, budgeting, facility support, electrical projects, lighting upgrades, and technology infrastructure. He also remains active in Oregon City civic leadership while continuing to support families throughout the region.

As a proud father, Josh is passionate about creating safe, structured, and empowering environments where young people can thrive. Recognizing Kate's exceptional talent, experience, and passion for teaching, he helped establish Evolve as a partnership built on trust, opportunity, and community. His focus is ensuring students and families have the support, resources, communication, and high-quality experience they deserve.
