About Evolve Dance and Fitness

Locally owned, values-forward, and built to serve Molalla-area families through dance, fitness, confidence, and community.

Mission

What Guides Us

To make dance and fitness accessible to local youth by building trust with families through a safe, uplifting environment, thoughtful communication, and age-appropriate programming, fostering confidence, strong character, and lifelong wellness.

Our story

Why We Started Evolve

Evolve Dance and Fitness was created to give Molalla-area families a local studio option focused on safe instruction, clear communication, age-appropriate programming, and a welcoming environment where students can grow as dancers and as people.

Our goal is to support families in Molalla, Oregon City, Canby, Mulino, Beavercreek, and surrounding South Clackamas County communities with programs that are organized, approachable, and family-centered.

Commitment to Families

We aim to provide thoughtful communication, consistent expectations, and a positive parent experience from registration through the season.

Age-Appropriate Programming

Class content, music, costuming, choreography, and presentations are intended to support a parent-trust standard.

Safety Standards

Studio procedures are built around safe movement, supervision, emergency information, incident reporting, and respectful conduct.

Community Focus

Evolve is built to serve local families and contribute to a positive youth enrichment environment in the Molalla area.
